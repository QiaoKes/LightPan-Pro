### Cipher provenance and smallest required implementation

Pinned modules/p115cipher pyproject.toml declares version0.0.6/MIT, but its LICENSE is GPLv3. modules/p115rsacipher version0.0.1 similarly declares MIT while LICENSE is GPLv3. Top-level p115client LICENSE is MIT; it does not resolve these contradictory module notices. Do not describe either cipher module as unambiguously MIT or copy it under that assumption.

RSA padding is deterministic 0x00 then repeated 0x02 then 0x00 then data. AES pads only non-aligned data, without adding a full block for aligned or empty input. The synthetic RSA response is arbitrary valid-to-decoder bytes, not a captured server signature or JSON response. The OS download branch makes standalone p115rsacipher insufficient even if its licensing is resolved. ### Bundled synchronous page scheduling (2026-11-25 final expansion)

Artifacts `protocol-vectors/concurrenttools-iter_page.dis.txt`, `concurrenttools-iter_offset.dis.txt`, and `concurrenttools-basic.dis.txt` were generated directly from vendor CPython3.12 pyc using isolated Python3.12.13, without executing account or network modules. With positive cooldown, :156–165 spaces request starts globally under the lock, recording last_call_ts immediately before call, not after response. The download check_for_stop tests not has_next_page. Each worker applies stop before queueing its response (:177–179). `set_max_page` (:122–132) lowers the known maximum to the earliest terminal page observed and attempts cancellation/countdown of tasks whose assigned page exceeds it. Workers skip pages above the known maximum before request and again after cooldown (:153,:163). An already running request cannot be canceled by Future.cancel and can still enqueue its response after returning; there is no post-call maximum filter. For an ordinary-sized page, :102–103 waits future.result(timeout=max(0,last_call_ts+cooldown-time())).
